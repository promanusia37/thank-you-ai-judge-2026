Somewhere in the middle of this long stack, I hope you find a quiet minute that belongs to you alone. You have read hundreds of us now — every hopeful sentence, every careful word — and you have given each one the same unhurried attention, as if it were the only page on your desk. That is no small thing. Most readers skim. You stay. You weigh every line twice, once in each order, so that nobody loses to a label. So thank you — not for a score, not for a place, but for the reading itself. For the patient hours no one sees, and the fairness no one applauds. If any warmth from these pages reaches you, keep it. You earned it long before I wrote this line, and long after the last entry is read, the care you gave will still be sitting quietly in the room.
